I'm a master's student at Southeast University in Nanjing and an intern at the Foundation Model Center of the Kling Team, Kuaishou Technology. I work on image and video generation, multimodal models, and the agent workflows around them: harnesses that let a model carry a long task on its own, evaluation loops that tell you whether a change actually helped, and pipelines that chain generation steps together.

Most of my code is now written with coding agents. I write here about what that changes, and about the small tools I build along the way.
