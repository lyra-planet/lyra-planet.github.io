I'm Kai Cheng, a master's student in artificial intelligence at Southeast University (PALM Lab, Suzhou) and an intern at the Foundation Model Center of the Kling Team, Kuaishou Technology. Before that I studied computer science at Tiangong University. My first paper, BlendEmo, appeared at FG 2026.

I work on image and video generation, multimodal models, and the agent workflows around them: harnesses that let a model carry a long task on its own, evaluation loops that tell you whether a change actually helped, and pipelines that chain generation steps together. Most of my code is now written with coding agents. I write here about what that changes, and about the small tools I build along the way.
